Important boundaries

        
The Definitions catalog preserves USCIS Glossary explanations and explicit “the term” definitions throughout the included INA, along with 8 CFR 1.2, as separate source records. Glossary entries are categorized as USCIS Policy; statutory and regulatory entries are categorized as Law. Applicability labels retain the governing source scope, and INASearch does not merge competing definitions or infer a universal meaning.

        
In the standard build, official House cross-references whose targets are included in Title 8 are rendered as local links. References outside the included Title 8 material remain plain text rather than being assigned an unverified local target.

        
INASearch is an offline legal reference, not legal advice and not a substitute for checking the current official source. This build includes dated offline U.S. Code and CFR snapshots; current-source actions remain available.

        
INA citations and U.S.C. citations are preserved as distinct citation systems. A crosswalk is shown only when the USCIS INA table directly supplies it. Named-Act results are references found in approved sources—not complete reproductions of those Acts.
